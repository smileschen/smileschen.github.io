# Hello!

apps vibe-coded by Miles Chen and Google Gemini

Here are a couple vibe-coded streamlit apps illustrating statistical concepts.

- [Confidence Interval for Proportion Simulator](https://smileschen.streamlit.app/)
- [Confidence Interval for Proportion Game](https://ci-proportion-game.streamlit.app/)
- [Hypothesis Testing for Proportions Game - Detect the cheating booth](https://boardwalk-ht.streamlit.app/)

# Linear regression

- [Least Squares Regression Explorer](https://smileschen.github.io/least-squares/) — adjust the intercept and slope to minimize the sum of squared residuals.

# Binary and floating-point numbers

- [Count in binary and hexadecimal](https://smileschen.github.io/binary-counter/) with an odometer that also shows fractional places.
- [Explore a miniature floating-point system](https://smileschen.github.io/floating-point/) by editing its bits and rounding real numbers on a number line.

# Random number generation

- [Explore a linear congruential generator](https://smileschen.github.io/random-number-generation/lcg/) and see how the multiplier, increment, modulus, and seed affect the sequence and its period.
- [Step through a miniature Mersenne Twister](https://smileschen.github.io/random-number-generation/mersenne-twister/) and see how seven five-bit words produce the next state and output.

# MCMC Animations

Step through multivariate MCMC with the same two-dimensional normal target. Each animation starts paused. Use **Next** to reveal each stage or **Play** for continuous playback.

- [Metropolis-Hastings with a uniform proposal](https://smileschen.github.io/mcmc/mh_2d_uniform.html)
- [Metropolis-Hastings with a normal proposal](https://smileschen.github.io/mcmc/mh_2d_normal.html)
- [Gibbs sampling with coordinate updates](https://smileschen.github.io/mcmc/gibbs_2d.html)

# Baseball models

Generate player abilities and hit counts, then use observed hits and at-bats to estimate individual abilities and the shared population mode.

- [Explore and compare Beta distributions](https://smileschen.github.io/beta-distribution/)
- [Explore Bayesian updating with a Beta prior](https://smileschen.github.io/bayesian-inference/)
- [Generate players from a fixed Beta prior](https://smileschen.github.io/baseball/beta_binomial_generation.html)
- [Generate a league from a hierarchical model](https://smileschen.github.io/baseball/hierarchical_beta_binomial_generation.html)
- [Infer player abilities from observed hits and at-bats](https://smileschen.github.io/baseball/baseball_hierarchical_inference.html)
- [Walk through four sweeps of Metropolis within Gibbs](https://smileschen.github.io/baseball/baseball_metropolis_within_gibbs.html)

# Coffee-bag filling machines

Generate bag weights from a Normal hierarchy, estimate the machine means from weighed bags, and step through four sweeps of Gibbs sampling.

- [Coffee-bag filling walkthrough](https://smileschen.github.io/coffee-filling/)

# Document clustering
