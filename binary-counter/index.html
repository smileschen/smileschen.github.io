Start at 15 and step once. The four rightmost bits roll from 1111 to 0000 as the next place picks up 16.

Count in sixteenths

To the right of the binary point, the places are 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, and 1/16. In 1010.1010, the fractional bits add 1/2 + 1/8 to 10.
