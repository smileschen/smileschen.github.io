      How this compares with MT19937

      Both generators join parts of two neighboring words, shift the result, apply a mask if the discarded bit was 1, and XOR with another word in memory. XOR compares the bits in each position. Two different bits give 1, and two equal bits give 0.

      MT19937 stores 624 words of 32 bits and uses a word 397 slots ahead. Here there are seven words of five bits, the offset is three slots, and the twist mask is 10101. This model also uses two shorter tempering steps.

      From the starting state, the first two outputs are both 13. On the first update the generator writes to A, and on the second it writes to B. The recurrence uses all 30 bits of six words and the high bit of the oldest word. With this mask and offset, those 31 bits, read in order from the pointer, cycle through all 231 − 1 nonzero states. MT19937 has period 219937 − 1.
