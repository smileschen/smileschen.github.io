    How the log score works

    Each word contributes the log weights of neighboring letters, including a space at each end, plus λ times its log weight in the lexicon. The pair weights come from War and Peace. An unknown word has weight 10−10.

    The word-by-word changes add up to the proposal’s score change, Δ. Moving λ starts a new score history with the displayed proposal as step 1.

    With a uniform key prior, this score is the log posterior up to a constant. Each proposal swaps two uniformly chosen entries. An improved score is always accepted, and a lower score can be accepted with probability eΔ.

    Decipher starts a fresh random key. Short passages can admit several plausible translations, and separate runs can find different readings.
